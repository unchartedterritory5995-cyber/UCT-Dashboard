# its hash was published must not be signed against the published value.

# `#!` directives under it are the same constraints in a form a machine reads, and

# recurring defect. A directive nobody can read is no better. So the reason stays in
# English, the constraint is derived, and a disagreement between the two is a refusal,

#   K CP2 before K CP3 - CP3 enforces constraints from inside CP2's own merge_all.py

#
# Syntax: `#!after: <stem> <- <stem>` means the LEFT unit merges AFTER the right one.
#         `#!last:  <stem>` marks the LAST MEMBER-VISIBLE unit, alone in its sitting.
#                   Rows after it must carry ZERO member-visible files, DERIVED from
