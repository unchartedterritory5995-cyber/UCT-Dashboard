How a day works

A day inside

  1. About 9:10 AM ET. The Zoom link drops in the live Zoom links channel.
  2. Before the open. Patrick (TSDR) and Bracco walk through the market, the levels and the names on their list.
  3. 9:30 AM ET. They trade the open live on screen share and talk through each decision.
  4. During the day. Each trader posts ideas and alerts in his own channel. Options flow and news run all day. Questions go in main chat.
  5. After the close. Every session is recorded, with a recap the same day. About 15 minutes catches you up.
  6. The weekend. Sunday Scans lands with the prep for the week ahead.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What time is the live session, and what if I can't watch?

The Zoom link drops about 20 minutes before the 9:30 AM ET open, every market day. Patrick (TSDR) and Bracco plan the day, then trade the open live. Can't make it? Every session is recorded, with a recap the same day.

Who are the traders?

Patrick (TSDR) and Bracco run the morning Zoom. ChartMaster is always easy to reach and runs our educational workshops. Alex Jones and a team of three skilled contributors run our robust options flow feed. Manrav and Jersace post ideas and alerts.

I'm new to trading. Is this for me?

Yes. You'll see how experienced traders plan the day and manage a trade, start with one or two traders whose style fits you, and get a free 1-on-1 call to get set up. Ask anything in main chat.

Is this signals to copy?

No. The traders post their ideas and alerts and explain the why, so you learn to make your own calls. Education only, not financial advice.

Stocks or options?

Both. The traders cover stocks and options, day trades and swings, and the options flow feed runs all day.

What do I need to get started?

A Discord account. After you join on Whop, you connect Discord, land in the room, and we set up a free 1-on-1 call to walk you through it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime in your Whop account before your next charge and you won't be charged again. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

What makes Uncharted Territory different?

The people. Every trader, contributor and member is helpful and easy to reach, the main chat stays focused on trading, and you watch experienced traders plan and manage real trades live, every market morning.
