Concept C — Selection + Command Strip

Track B · prototype ← all concepts

Click a label to select it. The row takes a gold rail, the plotted line itself lights up on the chart, and every other label dims — so you always know which line you are about to act on. The verbs appear in one strip in one fixed place, never beside the label. Shift-click adds to the selection and the strip acts on all of it — the only one of the three models that can hide or move four things at once. Esc clears.

What this concept claims

  • The strongest idea here is not the strip — it is that selection connects the label to its line. On a chart with eleven series that is the question a member actually has: which one is this?
  • Verbs never appear beside a label, so the legend has no hover chrome at all — the quietest resting state of the three.
  • It is the only model that scales to batch operations (hide four MAs, move three series into one pane).
  • Cost, stated: a click that only selects is a click that appears to do nothing, and the strip is far from the label — a long mouse trip for a one-off Hide. It wins on a busy chart and loses on a simple one.
