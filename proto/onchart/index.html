On-chart plot & indicator management

Track B · research

Three genuinely different interaction models for managing a plotted series directly from its label. All three share one resting label design and one set of canonical writers — they differ only in what hover, click and drag mean. Every fixture is the same: eleven instances across five panes, including a duplicate QQQ, a hidden MA, a three-plot MACD, a long indicator name and a plot that computes nothing.

click → popover

Concept A — Click Popover

The label is a button. One chevron on hover in a permanently reserved gutter; one compact popover of high-frequency verbs on click or right-click. Drag is secondary.

/a.html
drag → placement

Concept B — Direct Manipulation

The label is a handle. Drag onto a pane, onto a seam for a new pane, into the left Park dock to hide, into the Remove well to delete. Right-click is the accessible twin.

/b.html
click → select

Concept C — Selection + Command Strip

Click selects; the plotted line lights up and every other label dims. Verbs live in one strip in one fixed place. Shift-click acts on several series at once.

/c.html
Recommendation: Concept A as the spine, Concept B's drag and Concept C's selection glow folded in. They are not exclusive. A is the discoverable default; B's drag is a conventional accelerator both TC2000 and TradingView already teach; C's "selected label lights its line" is the single best idea for a chart carrying eleven series and costs one canvas redraw.
