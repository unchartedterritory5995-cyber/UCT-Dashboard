Concept B — Direct Manipulation

Track B · prototype ← all concepts

The label is a handle, not a button. Hover widens the colour rail into a grip. Drag is the primary verb: onto a pane to join it, onto a seam to make a new pane, into the left Park dock to hide it, into the Remove well to delete it. Click selects and flashes the line; double-click opens Chart Data; right-click is the short accessible twin of every drag. Try dragging SPY onto the RSI pane, or MA 20 off the price pane onto a seam.

What this concept claims

  • Placement is spatial, so it needs no menu, no words and no mental model — you put the line where you want it.
  • Both TC2000 and TradingView already do this; dragging a legend title between panes is an established convention, not a novelty.
  • Park (TC2000's "pin", refined) makes Hide a place rather than a state — a parked series is visibly still on the chart, one click from returning. This is the single best idea in TC2000.
  • Remove is a far corner you must travel to, which is what makes it feel deliberately destructive without a dialog.
  • Cost, stated: drag is undiscoverable on its own and unusable by keyboard. The right-click menu is not optional here — it is the twin that makes the model legal.
