# Third-party notices

Attribution for third-party source vendored into this repository, and for third-party
work whose *semantics* were used as a reference while implementing our own.

Policy: [`docs/pine/LICENSING.md`](docs/pine/LICENSING.md).

---

## Vendored source

*Nothing yet.*

This heading exists before the first entry on purpose. The moment a file is copied in is
exactly when attribution is easiest to skip and hardest to reconstruct later, so the
convention is here waiting rather than being invented under pressure.

When something is vendored, add a row with: what was taken, from where (project + URL +
commit), under which licence, where it now lives in this repo, and what was changed.

## Semantics referenced without copying code

*Nothing yet.*

Reading a permissively-licensed implementation to understand a behaviour — then writing
our own — is not vendoring and usually needs no notice. It is recorded here anyway when
the borrowed idea is substantial enough that a reader of our code would want to know
where it came from.

⛔ **AGPL projects are not read at all** (PineTS, piner, pyne, pinescription,
vela-pinets). Nothing about them belongs in this file, because nothing about them is
permitted to influence this codebase.
