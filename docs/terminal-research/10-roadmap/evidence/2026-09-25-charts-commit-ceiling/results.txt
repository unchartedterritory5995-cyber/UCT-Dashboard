## What it means
- The commits are the live tape repainting five chart widgets (15 canvases) at ~15 commits/s during the session.
  A loop does not care whether the network delivers ticks; this does. The 2026-09-10 loop the ceiling exists for
  ran ~4,500 commits per SECOND (~22,500 per 5 s) and froze navigation; here navigation passed everywhere.
- COMMIT_CEILING = 60 per 5 s was calibrated after hours (screener idle: 7). It cannot distinguish a live-market
  /charts board (60-90) from a loop (thousands) -- the same defect class the tool already fixed once for
  MUTATIONS (MUTATION_NOISY vs MUTATION_CEILING). Recalibration: commits > 60 -> reported as live-data NOISY;
  commits > 600 (120/s, 7x today's max live reading, 37x below the real loop) -> RENDER LOOP.
- CP2 (the change under test) touches no file /charts imports; this reading was never about it.
