## How it works

1. **Request path** (`blackline_snapshot`): reads the cache and nothing else. On a miss it returns `state: pending` and queues one refresh on the module's own single-thread `ThreadPoolExecutor` (`filing-blackline`, at most 8 queued). This is the same split `edgar_ownership` uses.
2. **Refresh** (`fetch_blackline`, on the worker only): ticker to CIK goes through `edgar.resolve_cik`. Every SEC read goes through `fundamentals_pit.sec_client.get_bytes`, which carries the declared UA, the process-wide limiter and backoff, with a 30s timeout and 1 retry. The refresh makes 3 requests: the submissions document and the two 10-K primary documents.
3. **Filings**: the two newest **original** `10-K` rows in `filings.recent`. A `10-K/A` is never compared, because an amendment is often a Part III-only document.
4. **Sections**: the HTML is flattened to blocks. Block tags end a block, table cells join within their row, and the `ix:header` is skipped. Each section has a start-heading regex and an end-heading regex:
   - `risk_factors`: `Item 1A … Risk Factors` → `Item 1B|1C|2`
   - `mdna`: `Item 7 … Management's Discussion` → `Item 7A|8`

   Only a block of 200 characters or fewer counts as a heading, so a sentence that merely mentions an item is not one. Every start match is a candidate, and the one with the **longest body** before the next end heading wins, which rejects the table-of-contents entry. A start heading with no end heading after it is not trusted. Running footers (`Apple Inc. | 2025 Form 10-K | 7`) and bare page numbers are dropped and **counted** per side (`furniture_dropped`).
5. **Diff**: `difflib` over the paragraph lists. Inside replaced runs, paragraphs are paired in order when their word-level similarity is ≥ 0.5, which makes them `changed` with word marks (`eq/del/ins` segments). Unpaired paragraphs are `added` or `removed`, and an exact removed+added pair becomes `moved`. Unchanged paragraphs are counted, not listed.
6. **Cache TTLs**: 12h when ok, 5 min when partial or failed, 1h when not_found.

## Measured on the fixtures (Apple FY2024 → FY2025 10-K)

- Risk Factors: 115 → 106 paragraphs. 9 added, 18 removed, 51 changed (0 boilerplate), 0 moved, 46 unchanged. 12 footers dropped per side.
- MD&A: 131 → 135 paragraphs. 26 added, 22 removed, 65 changed (**45 boilerplate**: "First Quarter 2024:" → "2025:", table figures), 44 unchanged.
- A hand-checked rewrite: "…results of operations and financial condition." became "…results of operations, financial condition and stock price."
- The trimmed fixtures produce the same counts as the full 1.5 MB documents, checked before trimming.
- Extraction plus diff of both full documents takes about 0.6 s on the worker.

## Open

- **10-Q pairs** are not built. Part II Item 1A in a 10-Q often only refers back to the 10-K, so a 10-Q slice needs its own "located but only a cross-reference" state.
- **Older submissions pages**: if the `recent` block lists fewer than two 10-Ks and `files` exist, the result is `not_found` with that stated reason, and the older pages are not read.
- **Heading coverage**: the extractor is proven on Apple's format only. Issuers that put "ITEM 1A" and "RISK FACTORS" in separate blocks, or that use a 10-K cross-reference index instead of item headings (GE-style), will come back `not_found`, which is the honest state. Measuring the not_found rate over a sample of tickers is the next step before arming.
- **Furniture rule**: bare 1–3 digit blocks are treated as page numbers. A table cell that is only a small number would also be dropped. It is counted, not hidden.
- **Not armed.** The owner decides whether to arm (`railway variables --service web --set FILING_BLACKLINE_ENABLED=1`). Not merged to `integrate/terminal-fixes` or master.
