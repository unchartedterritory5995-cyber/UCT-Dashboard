**Vocabulary.** TERMINAL-CURRENT = the `/calendar` surface, display-named "UCT Terminal" since 2026-09-01 (section E). TERMINAL-NEXT = the product this program designs. Brand: UT parent, UCT Intelligence product.

**How to read a row.** One row per existing capability. Every cell traces to a leaf report section (`D-nn §x`) and, where the leaf did, a `path:line`; production statements are CONFIRMED only by a log, health payload, scheduler entry, variable read (ORCH) or the 2026-09-02 production render — otherwise CLAIM. Contradictions between leaves are settled in `system-map.md` §12 (R1–R25); the ledger uses the resolved value and cites the R-number. Companion artifacts: `system-map.md` (gate 2), `tech-debt-register.md` (TD-nn, cited inline). Out of scope, cited by path: the provider ledger (`02-data-providers/provider-ledger.md`, F-03b) and the licensing register (`09-security-licensing-cost/licensing-register.md`, F-04).

**Column vocabularies (Part V, compressed).**
- **User** — desk (owner + internal traders) · member · staff (admin/ops) · pipeline (machine consumer).
- **Gate** — public · free · paid · admin · PUSH_SECRET · token (`CHART_RENDER_TOKEN`) · HMAC · partner (partner-owned file, existence recorded only).
