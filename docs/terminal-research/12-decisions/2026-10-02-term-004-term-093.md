## TERM-004 / OQ-14: one canonical earnings-date authority

### Decision

1. **`GET /api/calendar` is THE canonical earnings-date authority.** DEC-12 moves from
   RECOMMENDED to **DECIDED**. The authority is the EW/Finviz forward schedule for the
   current week and the Finnhub+FMP range weeks, with the Finnhub past-day backfill
   (`api/routers/calendar.py::_get_calendar_payload`, contract
   `api/services/calendar_week_contract.py`).
2. **The Discord bot's earnings path is SUPERSEDED, because it is unreachable.** It is a
   deletion candidate and has not been deleted (see "Deletion candidate" below).
3. **The real second authority is the ENGINE, not the bot.** It is live and it disagrees
   with `/api/calendar`. Its conformance is a follow-up, **TERM-004b**, owned in the
   engine repo. It is not done in this lane, for the reasons under "Why not built here".

row, and the stale row stays and is still served. Measured: **905 symbols carry more than

### Deletion candidate (recorded, NOT deleted)

- `C:\Users\Patrick\uct_intelligence\brain\retrieval.py:390-395` is the step-7 catalyst
  calendar call. It is the bot's only earnings-date read.
- Evidence that deleting it is safe: D above. The path is unreachable while the app's HTTP
  endpoint is set, and the commands it serves are not registered.
- Not deleted because the lane brief requires a branch in that repo and the repo has no
  git, so no branch can be cut. The lane's worktree isolation also refuses writes outside
  the dashboard worktree. The owner, or a session rooted in that directory, can act on this
  record.
- ⚠️ If the bot is ever revived, it must take its dates from `/api/calendar`, using the
  TERM-004b adapter below. Today it would serve the engine's dates, and 31% of those
  disagree with the dashboard.

### TERM-004b: conform the engine (follow-up, engine repo)

The adapter is one function. `uct_intelligence/api.py::get_catalyst_calendar_context`
stops querying `earnings_analytics` for **upcoming** dates. Instead it reads
`GET {DASHBOARD_URL}/api/calendar?week=<monday>` for each week overlapping
`[today, today+days_ahead]`. That surface already exists, is anonymous by design, and is
railed: `tests/test_open_reads_gate.py::test_iv_the_anonymously_read_reference_data_stays_ANONYMOUS`
lists `/api/calendar`. The function collects `days[date][bmo|amc|tbd][].sym` for the
requested symbols and keeps the existing output format. The news half is unchanged.
Parity test (engine repo): given a recorded `/api/calendar` payload, the function's
UPCOMING EARNINGS lines equal the payload's dates for the requested symbols. Moving one
date in the fixture must move the output (the FB-A5-01 "known it worked" test).
⛔ Do not add a second scraper, and do not fix the INSERT OR IGNORE by adding another
vendor. Historical `earnings_analytics` rows (beats, surprises) stay where they are. Only
the forward date stops being authored there.
No dashboard endpoint change is needed. A dashboard-side test was not added because the
consumer contract (anonymous, shape-asserted) is already railed by
`test_open_reads_gate.py` and `test_calendar_week_contract.py`.

### What would reverse it

Finding the engine's FMP forward dates more accurate than `/api/calendar` on a measured
sample of reported outcomes. That means comparing the actual report day against both
predictions, and no such sample exists. Until then the 201 disagreements count against the
engine, because its rows cannot be corrected (B).

## TERM-093: the "what else was open, named" capture

### Decision: the subject

**The owner-desk, operationalised as the admin role.** Basis:
- The tool fact is owner-stated (2026-09-26: *"Assume that i personally use every other
  site mentioned"*, `04-workflows/workflow-library.md` §5.1). The **step** is the unmeasured
  part. The person whose step it is, is the subject.
- §5.4 item 2 asks *"Does the **owner** open finviz.com by hand for chart inspection
  (WF-C13)?"*. CARD 24 says no code read can answer that, and CP-06 records the owner
  declining to itemise it.
- Members are never asked. The TERM-093 risks bind here: 13 accounts, six of them admins;
  `n` is reported as `n`; the ~750 Whop members are another product's and are out of
  boundary.

### Decision: the occasion

**Opening the breadth drill (`BreadthDrillModal`), declared as `wf_c13_breadth_drill`.**
WF-C13's sequence starts there: "click a breadth cell → BreadthDrillModal opens". It is a
click a person made, so it is not a change callback. WF-C09's occasion, TickerPopup, is
the same posture. It was left out of the smallest version and is a one-line mount plus one
entry in `OCCASIONS` if the first reading warrants it.

### What was built (dark)

- `api/services/what_else_open.py` creates a lazy `what_else_open_occasions` table in
  auth.db. It keeps three states apart (absent / unanswered / answered), writes each answer
  once, and has no DELETE.
- `api/routers/what_else_open.py` has three `require_admin` routes, all 404 while the flag
  is unset: `POST /api/instruments/what-else-open/occasion`,
  `POST …/occasion/{id}/answer`, and `GET /api/admin/instruments/what-else-open` (the
  recorded/eligible read-back, with `n_subjects`).
- `app/src/components/instruments/WhatElseOpenPrompt.jsx` is mounted in
  `BreadthDrillModal`. It is admin-only and renders nothing on the 404. The choices are
  finviz.com · TradingView · thinkorswim · Other · Nothing else. Skip leaves the occasion
  unanswered.
- The flag `WHAT_ELSE_OPEN_CAPTURE_ENABLED` is registered `pending` in
  `docs/feature_flags.json`. It is pending because arming it starts recording rows about a
  named person, so the subject arms it.
- Not touched: Notebook (`pages/journal-2-0/**`), `app/src/hub/**`, and the chart engine.

### What would reverse it

The owner naming a different subject, or retiring the question. Reversal is unsetting the
flag. Rows already given are never deleted.
