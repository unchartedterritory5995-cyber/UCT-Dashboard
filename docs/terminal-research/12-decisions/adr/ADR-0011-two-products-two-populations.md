# ADR-0011 — Two products, two populations — the Whop Discord is outside this programme's boundary

**STATUS: ACCEPTED** · 2026-09-26 · decided by the OWNER · gate item 13, 15, 27

**Why it is an ADR and not a tracker row:** Locked hard: an owner statement of fact about his own products, with a scope consequence the cards state as binding on every downstream document.

**Supersedes:** every prior use of "~750 members" as a UCT Intelligence population

## Context

The programme had been quoting *"under ~750 members in the community server"* from
`00-program-control/charter/OWNER_SEED_FACTS.md:61` without knowing what the figure counted.

## Decision

✅ **Owner, verbatim, 2026-09-26: "We have 750 members in discord paying."**
(`12-decisions/DECISION_CARDS_2026-09-26.md:676`)

⛔ **It is the Whop product's population and is NOT UCT Intelligence's.** UCT Intelligence is
**~26 accounts**, **13** with any page-view row, six of the roster admins.
**Two products, two populations, never one denominator.** (`:694`)

⛔ **SCOPE CONSEQUENCE:** *"The Whop live-trading Discord is a separate product and is out of
this programme's boundary. Any document that counts Whop members, Whop revenue or Whop content as
UCT Intelligence's has crossed a product line."* (`:641`)

## Alternatives actually considered

1. **Treat ~750 as the member base.** Rejected by the owner's own sentence — and it would
   have put a 29× error into every per-member figure in the cost model.
2. **Treat the two as a union.** ⚠️ **NOT AUDITED, and this ruling creates the
   question rather than closing it:** whether the ~750 community-server figure and the
   paid-Substack-audience reasoning **in the licensing register** describe the Whop audience, the
   UCT Intelligence audience, or a union of both (`:641`). That is an open question (gate item
   33), not a decision.

## Consequences

* ⭐⭐ **It unblocks CARD 22's blocker.** The definition-of-done panel ruled the owner
  cannot supply the verdict on his own product, and the honest fallback was
  INCONCLUSIVE-BY-CONSTRUCTION. **There are 750 people already paying for a sibling product** —
  the subject pool is not empty; it is large, reachable and already transacting. ⚠️
  Naming one is still owner-only, and the panel's requirement is unchanged, but *"there isn't
  one" is no longer the likely answer* (`:696`). See ADR-0033.
* `GOVERNING_PRINCIPLES.md:78` carries the correction.
* Every per-member cost or adoption figure in the programme must state which product's
  denominator it uses.
