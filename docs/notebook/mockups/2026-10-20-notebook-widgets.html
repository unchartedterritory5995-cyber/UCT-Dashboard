What I'd build first, and what I'm leaving out

Claude's call
  • Wave 1: the "/" menu with the new widgets, the ticker card, My watchlist today, My names reporting, My P&L and stats, My open positions and risk.
  • Wave 2: My rules check and My recurring mistakes. These need two small new server reads (rules kept over time, mistakes over time).
  • Wave 3: Market on my names, options flow on a ticker, and starter styles that come with widgets already placed.
  • Later: putting the existing widgets (calendar, watchlist, themes...) in the "/" menu too. Each needs its own fill-on-insert step, so it waits.
  • Left out: exposure scores, the econ calendar (it's off), the morning wire and trader ledger (paid-only).
  • Without a linked broker: account widgets read manual and CSV trades, and say so.
