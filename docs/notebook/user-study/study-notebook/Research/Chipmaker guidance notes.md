What I watch when a chipmaker reports.

- Guidance for the next quarter moves these stocks more than the quarter just reported.
- Inventory days at distributors: a build-up usually comes before a cut in orders.
- Data-center demand and consumer demand are two different businesses now; read them separately.
- Gross margin tells me whether the mix is getting better or the discounts are getting deeper.
