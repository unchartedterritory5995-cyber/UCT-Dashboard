CrowdStrike, which trades as CRWD, sells endpoint security as a subscription platform.

## What I think matters

- Net new recurring revenue is the number the market reacts to, more than the headline beat.
- The platform story depends on customers adding modules, so the module adoption figures matter.
- An outage or a security incident is the risk that would change the story fastest.

## How I would trade it

- Only after a base forms on declining volume; I do not buy extended moves in this name.
- The stop goes under the base low. If it breaks, the thesis waits for a new base.
