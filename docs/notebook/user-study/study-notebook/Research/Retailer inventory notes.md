---
title: "Retailer inventory notes"
tags: [research, consumer]
---
Inventory is the tell in retail.

- Inventory growing faster than sales means discounts are coming.
- Inventory falling while sales hold means cleaner margins ahead.
- Watch what management says about promotions; they rarely say "discount" out loud.
- The reaction on the first day after the report often reverses within a week in this group.
