Why software stocks re-rate on margins and not only on growth.

- Operating margin expanding while growth holds up is the combination the market pays for.
- Stock-based compensation hides real cost; I look at margin with it included.
- Free cash flow margin is harder to dress up than operating margin.
- A slowdown in new customers shows up in billings before it shows up in revenue.
