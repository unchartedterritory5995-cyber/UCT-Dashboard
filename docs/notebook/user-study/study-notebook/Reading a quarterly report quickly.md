title: "Reading a quarterly report quickly"
tags: [research, process]
---
My ten-minute pass over a quarterly filing.

1. Revenue line and its growth against the same quarter a year earlier.
2. Gross margin, and whether it moved more than a point.
3. Cash from operations against net income: a big gap needs an explanation.
4. Inventory and receivables growing faster than revenue.
5. The risk factors section: anything new since the last filing.

Pull the lines that matter into the note with their page, so I can find them again.
