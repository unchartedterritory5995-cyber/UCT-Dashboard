---
title: "Position sizing rules"
tags: [playbook, risk]
---
The rules I do not break, however good the setup looks.

1. Risk a fixed fraction of the account per trade, measured from entry to the stop.
2. Half size when the market is choppy or the setup is not my best pattern.
3. No new position larger than the smallest one I am already struggling with.
4. The stop goes in when the order goes in. Not later.

==If I cannot say where I am wrong, the position is too big.==
