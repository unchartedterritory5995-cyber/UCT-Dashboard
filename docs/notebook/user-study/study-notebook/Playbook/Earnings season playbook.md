How I handle the reporting weeks, written down so I stop relearning it every quarter.

## Before the report

- Know whether the name reports before the open or after the close.
- Cut the position to a size I can hold through a gap either way.
- Write the one number that would change my mind, before the report, not after.

## After the report

- The first thirty minutes are noise. Let the opening range form.
- A gap up that holds its opening range low is information. A gap up that fills is also information.
- If the reaction contradicts the numbers, trust the reaction and find out why later.
