What gets a name onto the watchlist.

- Relative strength against the index over the last few months.
- A tight base: the range narrowing, the volume drying up.
- Enough daily volume that my size is a rounding error.
- A reason people care: a new product, a turn in the numbers, a theme with money behind it.
