How I decide how aggressive to be.

- When most stocks are above their longer moving averages, breakouts work; I press.
- When breadth is narrowing while the index rises, I tighten stops and take partial profits.
- When breakouts fail on the first retest several times in a row, I stop buying breakouts.
- The regime changes before the headlines do.
