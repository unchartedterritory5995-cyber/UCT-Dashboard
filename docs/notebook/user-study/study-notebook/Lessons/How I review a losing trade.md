---
title: "How I review a losing trade"
tags: [lessons, process]
---
Four questions, in this order, before I look at the next chart.

1. Was it a setup from my playbook, or something I talked myself into?
2. Was the size inside my [[Position sizing rules]]?
3. Did I exit where I planned, or did I move the stop?
4. Would I take the same trade again with the same information?

If the answers are yes, yes, yes and yes, the loss was the cost of doing business.
