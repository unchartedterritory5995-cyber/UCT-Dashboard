# UCT Notebook study — consent

> **DRAFT — owner approval required.** This text is not used with any participant until the
> owner has approved it (the same rule as the email-in Privacy sentence). The facilitator reads
> it aloud or sends it before the session, and keeps the signed or emailed "I agree".

Thank you for helping us test the UCT Notebook. Please read this before we start. You can ask
anything, and you can stop at any time.

## What we are testing

The product, not you. There are no right answers. If something is confusing, that is exactly
what we need to hear.

## What we record

- **Your screen and your voice** during the 45-minute call, so we can review what happened.
- **Your answers** to a short questionnaire (ten questions, 1 to 5).
- **Our notes**: which tasks worked, how long they took, and what you said.

We do **not** record your camera. We do not record anything outside the call.

## How long we keep it

- The **recording is deleted after 30 days**.
- In our notes and results you are **P1 to P8**, never your name. Nothing we publish or share
  names you.

## The account you use

- We give you a **fresh test account**, set up for this study. It is not your own UCT
  Intelligence account.
- **Please do not paste real positions, account numbers or personal details** into it. Use
  made-up notes or the sample notes we load for you.
- After the study the account is **closed**, or kept only if you ask to keep it (you choose
  below).

## After the call: usage counts for 30 days

If you keep using the test account after the call, the Notebook records **counts and timings**
— for example how many notes were opened and how long a note took to open — for **30 days**, as
part of a reliability check we run with a small group of traders. It **never records what you
write**: the server keeps only a fixed list of numbers and short labels for each event, and
throws away anything else. You can say no to this and still take part in the call.

## Stopping

You can stop at **any time**, during or after the call, without giving a reason, and you
**keep the thank-you** either way. If you stop after the call, tell us and we delete your
recording and notes early.
