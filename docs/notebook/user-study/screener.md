# UCT Notebook study — recruiting and scheduling

Part of the study kit (`../user-study-kit.md`). Recruit **6–8 traders** so that at least five
complete a session.

## 1. The recruit post — paste into the community Discord

```
Looking for 6–8 traders for a 45-minute call about the UCT Notebook (the research notebook inside UCT Intelligence).

You'd use it on your own screen while thinking out loud: write a note, save something from an article, find an old note, ask it a question. No prep, no right answers. We're testing the product, not you.

Who: anyone who keeps trading notes today, in Notion, Evernote, Obsidian, OneNote, a doc or on paper. A mix of those is exactly what we want.
When: [dates]. Pick a slot here: [link]
Thank-you: [incentive]

Reply here or DM me with the five screener answers below.
```

## 2. The screener (five questions)

1. **What do you keep trading notes in today?** Notion · Evernote · Obsidian · OneNote · a doc ·
   paper · something else (which?)
2. **How do you trade?** Day trading · swing trading · longer-term · a mix
3. **About how many notes do you write in a week?** 0–2 · 3–10 · more than 10
4. **Which devices would you use for notes?** Computer only · computer and phone · phone only
5. **Your phone:** iPhone (which iOS version, if you know) · Android · none

**Aim for:** at least two current Notion users, one Obsidian user, one Evernote user and one
paper/doc person; at least three with a phone (T9 needs one; iOS 16 or newer is the product's
declared floor).

**Exclude** anyone who has helped build or test the product.

## 3. Scheduling

- 45 minutes on a video call with screen sharing, on a **computer** (the phone is used only
  in T9).
- Book no more than **two sessions a day**, with at least 30 minutes between them: the
  facilitator provisions a fresh account and runs the pre-flight before each one
  (`facilitator-guide.md`).
- Send the confirmation with the date, the time **and its time zone**, the call link, and the
  consent form (`consent.md`) to read beforehand.

## 4. Reminder — 24 hours before

```
Reminder: our 45-minute UCT Notebook session is tomorrow at [time, time zone]: [call link].
Please be on a computer, and have your phone nearby for one short task. The consent form is here: [link] — reply "I agree" if you haven't yet.
```

## 5. After the session — thank-you and incentive

- Send the thank-you **the same day**, with the incentive, whatever happened in the session —
  including when the participant stopped early (the consent form promises this).
- Record in the incentive log only the P number and "sent" — never the incentive details next
  to results.

## 6. No-shows

- Wait **10 minutes**, then send one message: "Still able to join? If not, no problem — want to
  pick another slot?"
- One reschedule is offered. A second no-show closes that invitation; the account provisioned
  for them is closed the same day (`facilitator-guide.md`, "After").
- A no-show is **not** a participant: nothing goes in the results for them, and their P number
  is given to the next person who completes a session.
