# Head-to-head benchmark: the protocol (UCT vs Notion vs Evernote vs Obsidian)

Wave 9, lane 9A, item A4. This is the method the owner follows, by hand, in one sitting on one
machine, to time the same five things (open, search, type, paste, a large note) in four note apps
with ONE instrument. The data sheet for the sitting is [`datasheet.md`](datasheet.md); the machine
record is a filled copy of [`machine.json`](machine.json); the results are
[`results.md`](results.md), which a tool writes.

## 0. Honesty first

- ⛔ **No number exists until the owner runs this.** Nothing in this repository is a competitor
  timing. `results.md` is GENERATED by `tools/notebook_bench_report.py` from the saved dumps and is
  never typed; its rail regenerates it and fails on a hand-typed number.
- ⛔ **No agent holds an account for Notion, Evernote or Obsidian.** The owner times them. The UCT
  column is also timed BY HAND, on the production bench account, with the same steps (ruling
  D-9A3). The automated sandbox row (`tools/notebook_bench_uct.py`) is a per-release cross-check on
  a loopback sandbox, never the UCT column and never ratioed against a hand cell.
- ⛔ **A probe that has not passed its self-test times nothing** (R-HON). The report refuses any
  dump without a passing `selfTest`, from another probe version, or from a machine the dump's user
  agent contradicts.
- **Internal only** until the owner has checked each vendor's terms on publishing benchmarks
  (ruling D-9A6).

## 4. DevTools discipline (every op, every round, every app)

1. Put the app in the op's start state (§5).
2. Open DevTools (F12; Obsidian: Ctrl+Shift+I), Console. Paste the WHOLE probe file. (Chrome may
   first ask you to type `allow pasting`.) It prints `uct-bench-probe/1 installed`.
3. `await __uctBench.selfTest()` must print **PASS**. On FAIL: note it in the data sheet, reload
   the app, paste again. Never time on a FAIL.
4. `__uctBench.check("<marker or title>")` must print **absent** (your console lines name it).
5. The op's `arm` line, from your console lines.
6. **CLOSE DevTools** (an open DevTools costs time on every frame -- risk R11).
7. Do the reps exactly as §5 says. Mouse for clicks, one crisp click, hands off until it has
   happened; scroll with the wheel only (a click on a scrollbar is a gesture).
8. Reopen DevTools. `__uctBench.status()` should show `attempts` equal to the reps (typing:
   `samples` equal to 60).
9. `copy(__uctBench.dump('<app>', '<op>'))` and save the clipboard as
   `runs/<run-id>/<app>__<op>__r<round>.json` (`<app>` is `notion`, `evernote`, `obsidian` or `uct`).
10. A reload loses the probe: paste it again and re-run `selfTest()` before the next arm.

## 5. The ops

| op | what is timed | kind | rounds × reps |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | open a small note in-app (warm) -> first content visible | open, first | 2 × 10 |
| H2 | open the 1,000-paragraph note -> first content visible | open, first | 2 × 10 |
| H2-full | open the 1,000-paragraph note -> fully in DOM (its last paragraph is present) | open, full | 2 × 10 |
| H3 | open the 2,000-paragraph note -> first content visible | open, first | 2 × 10 |
| H3-full | open the 2,000-paragraph note -> fully in DOM (its last paragraph is present) | open, full | 2 × 10 |
| H4 | search the rare term -> the expected note's row appears | search | 2 × 10 |
| H5 | quick switcher by unique title -> the row appears | search | 2 × 10 |
| H6 | typing per character at the end of the small note | typing | 3 × 60 characters |
| H7 | typing per character at the end of the 2,000-paragraph note | typing | 3 × 60 characters |
| H8 | paste the 200-paragraph payload into an empty note -> its end marker renders | paste | 2 × 5 |
| H9 | cold start: reload -> largest contentful paint (LCP proxy), reported separately | cold | 5 × 1 |

"First content visible" is the headline for opens; "fully in DOM" sits beside it where the editor
renders the whole note (ruling D-9A2). An editor that virtualises its content may never hold the
last paragraph: the probe then records `not-in-dom` after 15 s, never a number.

### The rotation (round r visits the apps in a rotated order, so drift spreads across all four)

| round | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| r1 | notion | evernote | obsidian | uct |
| r2 | evernote | obsidian | uct | notion |
| r3 | obsidian | uct | notion | evernote |
| r4 | uct | notion | evernote | obsidian |
| r5 | notion | evernote | obsidian | uct |

The sitting goes op by op (H1 … H9); inside an op, round by round; inside a round, app by app in
the row's order. One (op, round, app) is one dump.

### H1 / H2 / H3 (first) and H2-full / H3-full (full): open a note

The target is `timed_notes.small` (H1), `large_1000` (H2), `large_2000` (H3), all in the `Bench`
folder / notebook. The probe starts a rep on the click that opens the target while the target is
NOT displayed, and treats a click made while it IS displayed as navigation away from it.

| app | start state | one rep | between reps |
|---|---|---|---|
| UCT | All notes (or the Bench folder), the target's card visible | click the card (`[data-note-card-id]`, `NoteCard.jsx:126`) | Alt+Left (browser Back) to the list |
| Notion | the Bench page, the target in the sidebar tree | click the page in the sidebar | Alt+Left back to the Bench page |
| Evernote | the Bench notebook: list + note panel, `Bench neutral note` shown | click the target's row | click `Bench neutral note` (navigation) |
| Obsidian | file explorer, Bench expanded, `Bench neutral note` open | click the target file | click `Bench neutral note` (navigation) |

Full mode (H2-full, H3-full): after each click keep hands off until the note is shown AND 15 s have
passed if its last paragraph never appears (that is the probe's `not-in-dom` timeout), then
navigate.

### H4: search the rare term

Type the manifest's `markers.rare_term` into the app's search at a natural pace (never paste it,
never press Enter). A rep starts on the first key typed into an EMPTY search field and ends when
the row titled `timed_notes.rare.title` appears; clear the field between reps.

| app | search field | clear between reps |
|---|---|---|
| UCT | the sidebar's Search tab (`Search notes`), then the box `Search your notes` (`FolderSidebar.jsx:1282-1287`, `:1310`) | Escape clears the query (`FolderSidebar.jsx:1312`) |
| Notion | the Search dialog (verify at run time) | Escape; reopen |
| Evernote | the search box (verify at run time) | clear the box |
| Obsidian | the Search pane (Ctrl+Shift+F; verify at run time) | Ctrl+A then Delete |

### H5: quick switcher by unique title

Type the manifest's `switcher_title` into the app's quick switcher. Same rules as H4.

| app | open the switcher | close between reps |
|---|---|---|
| UCT | Ctrl+K (`CommandPalette.jsx:150`; input `Search a security, company, or note`, `:534`) | Escape |
| Notion | its quick-find shortcut (verify at run time) | Escape |
| Evernote | its note switcher, if it has one; otherwise its search, recorded as a deviation (verify at run time) | Escape / clear |
| Obsidian | Ctrl+O (quick switcher; verify at run time) | Escape |

### H6 / H7: typing at the end of a note

Open the small note (H6) or the 2,000-paragraph note (H7) and let it finish loading. Arm, close
DevTools, click in the note's LAST paragraph, press Ctrl+End, then type `x` at a steady pace until
at least 60 have landed (holding the key down to auto-repeat is fine; the probe keeps the first
60). Fewer than 60 samples makes that dump INCONCLUSIVE -- the report never takes a p95 over the
keys that happened to land. Three rounds, three dumps.

### H8: paste the 200-paragraph payload

Open `paste-payload.html` from the corpus in the clean Chrome, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C (this puts HTML on
the clipboard). Arm once per round; then per rep: create a NEW empty note (UCT: `+ New note`,
`NotebookTab.jsx:1894`; the other apps: their new-note command, verify at run time), click into its
body, Ctrl+V, wait until the paste has landed, and move on. Close any earlier pasted note first (an
end marker still in the page makes the next rep INVALID).

### H9: cold start (LCP proxy)

Per round: close DevTools, reload the app (Chrome: F5; Obsidian: the command "Reload app without
saving", verify at run time), wait until the note list shows, then open DevTools, paste the probe
and run **`await __uctBench.arm('cold')` BEFORE clicking anything in the page** (an input finalises
the largest-contentful-paint entry), then `await __uctBench.selfTest()`, then the dump. It is
labelled "LCP proxy" everywhere: a probe pasted after load cannot see navigation start any other
way. Five rounds, five dumps.

## 7. Threats to validity (read before quoting any number)

- **Network.** Notion, Evernote and UCT production pay network round trips; Obsidian is local. The
  automated UCT row is loopback and pays none, which is why it is never ratioed against a hand cell.
- **Virtualising editors** may never hold the last paragraph of a long note: `full` reads
  `not in DOM`, so `first` is the comparable headline (D-9A2).
- **Warm vs cold.** H1-H8 run warm (the app loaded, the list shown); H9 is a cold-ish reload, and
  LCP is the LARGEST paint -- in UCT that may be the brand intro that plays on every load, not the
  usable list. The dump names the LCP element.
- **Where t0 is.** An open's t0 is the pointerdown. An app that opens on `click` includes the press
  (pointerdown to pointerup, ~50-150 ms by hand), one that opens on pointerdown does not; the dump
  carries each rep's press beside its sample. A sample ENDS at the MutationObserver callback after
  the task that inserted the marker (before paint), not at a paint.
- **The probe's own cost** is a MutationObserver plus a text scan of the inserted content -- a few
  ms on a 2,000-paragraph note, the same for every app.
- **Search is not one thing**: a title-only switcher and a full-text search answer different
  questions; H4 and H5 keep them apart, and an app without a switcher is a recorded deviation.
- **Imports differ**: Evernote flattens subfolders into seven notebooks; Notion gets no tags or
  dates; Obsidian reads the front matter; UCT imports the vault. Indexing lag is why the rare term
  is proved first.
- **Plans and regions**: a free plan, a server region or the time of day can move a cloud app's
  numbers; record them (`machine.json`, data sheet).
- **One person, one sitting, one machine**: human timing varies, so reps and the rotation spread
  it; the box's memory is read before and after every round.
- **The corpus is synthetic** and has no images, tables or code (D-9A1); a real library is not.
