# UCT Notebook — Competitive Gap Ledger

**This is a living tracking artifact, not a research report.** The research lives in
`competitive-primary-platform-phase-zero.md` (12 dispatches, initial competitive
research) and `competitive-primary-platform-phase-one-adversarial.md` (9 independent
adversarial reviews that corrected, rescoped, and re-prioritized Phase Zero — read
Phase One's verdict, not Phase Zero's original claim, wherever the two disagree).
This ledger's job is narrower and more durable: **one row per capability gap, with a
STATUS that gets updated as work ships**, so "what do we still need to match Notion/
Evernote/Obsidian" has one current answer instead of requiring a re-read of two
613/564-line research documents every time.

**Baseline:** Phase Zero/One research ran 2026-09-05, against commit `c882f426d`.
This ledger's STATUS column reflects **2026-09-06** reality — Waves 0-3 (Trust
Foundation, Capture Completion, Ask Current Note, Thesis-Trade Link), Stage A
validation instrumentation, and Wave 4 prep all shipped or were designed in the day
between that research and this ledger, verified this session via 3 fresh read-only
research passes, not assumed from the wave names alone.

**Update discipline:** change STATUS only when code/evidence changes, not on a
schedule. Add new rows for newly-discovered gaps; never delete a row — mark it
DONE/REJECTED/SUPERSEDED and keep the history.

---

## Legend

- **Parity/Diff:** PARITY (table stakes, must match) · DIFF (financial-native
  differentiation, no competitor equivalent) · BOTH.
- **Status:** OPEN · IN PROGRESS · DONE (cite the commit/wave) · PARTIAL (some done,
  real scope remains) · REJECTED (per Phase One's Do-Not-Build) · BLOCKED (external
  dependency, e.g. legal review) · EXPERIMENT (validate-first, not yet scheduled).
- **Priority:** as currently assigned by Phase One's revised tiers, carried forward
  unless a later decision-log entry changed it (cited if so).
