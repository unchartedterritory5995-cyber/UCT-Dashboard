## ⭐⭐ The finding of this run: the INSTRUMENT was losing cells, not the product

P2 kept coming back INCONCLUSIVE and the obvious explanation was deploy churn.
It was not. Three separate instrument defects were found and fixed, each with a
measured before/after.

### 1. Sampling where a waiter was available — **the big one**

The cell checked for the editor with `query_selector('.ProseMirror')` every 5
seconds. An editor that mounted at t=8 s went unseen until t=12 s; one at t=34 s
was never seen, and the cell died on *"the editor never mounted"* while the
product had been ready for seconds.

| the SAME ticker cell | GREEN | INCONCLUSIVE | seconds |
|---|---|---|---|
| sampling (`query_selector` + sleep) | 3 | 3 | 748 |
| **waiting (`wait_for_selector`)** | **6** | **0** | **211** |

⭐ **Six of six, and 3.5× faster — by changing HOW it waited, not what it
measured.** The ceiling was left identical, so nothing that used to pass could
start failing.

⛔ **And it was about to be blamed on deploy churn.** The swap detector recorded
**zero** swap-waits for that window — production was stable throughout. Only an
independent signal separated "the box swapped" from "my instrument blinked".


that as "it hung at startup".** It had not — the rig Chrome's creation timestamp
(08:03:39 against a run beginning 08:03:36) proves it came up normally three
seconds in. **The absence of evidence was an artefact of the handler.**

Two defects, and fixing one was insufficient: the child was **also**
block-buffering into the pipe. Both fixed.
