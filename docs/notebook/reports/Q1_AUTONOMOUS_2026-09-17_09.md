**2. ⛔ `--max-workers 1` was my mitigation and it was the wrong theory.** I
reasoned the kills were vitest exhausting memory, so halving concurrency should
help. Attempts 3 and 4 printed *nothing*, and attempt 4 died with more headroom
than attempt 1 survived to use — so the killer fires before the gate allocates
anything. **The mitigation addressed a mechanism that was not the one operating.**

standby list, so it under-reports what a process can allocate;
`Memory\Available MBytes` is the number. They agreed closely here (4.44 vs 4.53 GB)
**only because the standby list happened to be small (0.34 GB)** — precisely the
agreement that teaches you to trust the wrong instrument. Both the correction and
that caveat are now in `CLAUDE.md`.

**4. ⚠️ The leak was not a one-off and "reaped" is not "fixed".** It regrew within
minutes of the first reap (2 procs at 3 min, 4 by the next check). `breadth-dc`
has a teardown bug; reaping is symptomatic relief that must be re-done and must
