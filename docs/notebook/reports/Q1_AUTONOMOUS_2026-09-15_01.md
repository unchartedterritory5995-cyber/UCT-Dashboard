## 5. Wave recommendation — LEAVE ON

**Evidence for it, not preference:**

- The loss requires a **specific and uncommon sequence**: type offline → leave the
  note → send a widget embed from a `/charts` widget → return. It is not the
  ordinary offline typing path.
- The **ordinary paths are GREEN**: all 24 metadata-door cells, and the whole
  offline unit layer (27 files / 372 tests).
- **Flipping the kill switch is not free.** Per R1, read from committed code:
  `useDurableNote.js:111/:160` both say *"OFF STOPS PROCESSING — it has never been
  permission to delete or alter what a member already wrote"*, `deleteDatabase()`
  is never called, and even a `BLOCKED` outbox entry is *"still stored"*. But a
  dark period **freezes the drain** and the *"edit it again to sync"* surface is
  gated identically — so already-queued work would be **stranded silently**, with
  no affordance telling anyone. That is a different harm, not an absence of harm.
- **2.8a was CLEAN** on fix 4's deploy: 985 status-bearing lines in the first 15
  minutes, all 2xx, none ≥ 500.

⛔ **The door guard (Phase 1) is the right mitigation and it was not built.** Until
it ships, the exposure stands. If the owner wants the exposure closed before a
fresh session can build the guard, **that** is when to reconsider the switch — and
it remains RESERVED either way.
