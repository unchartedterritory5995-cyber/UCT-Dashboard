## 3.4 · Every path that acquires a render slot, with its class

There are **exactly two `RENDER_SLOTS.acquire` sites in production code.** Everything below reaches
one of them. (Test files hold seven more; they are not paths.)

| # | site | reached from | trigger | **class** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | `discord_interactions.py:1226` (`produce_chart`) | `run_chart_job:1864` ← router `:524`, `:636` | member runs **`/chart`** (V1) | **MEMBER** |
| **A** | ″ | `run_multi_chart_job:1357` ← router `:422`, `:551`, `:568` | member runs **`/charts`** (V1) | **MEMBER** |
| **A** | ″ | `run_chart_job` / `run_multi_chart_job` ← `discord_render/commands.py:528,533,543,553,563` | member's **V2** job, off the queue | **MEMBER** |
| **A** | ″ | `_warm_hot_charts:1628`, `slot_wait=MULTI_SLOT_WAIT_S` | **the hot-chart warm cycle**, on a scheduler | ⛔ **BACKGROUND** |
| **A** | ″ | `heal_stand_in:1698` ← `schedule_stand_in_heal:1913` | **stand-in self-heal**, 45 s / 120 s AFTER the member already has a chart | ⛔ **BACKGROUND** |
| **B** | `buzz_image.py:252` (`_render_uncached`) | `routers/discord_interactions.py:152` | member runs **`/buzz`** | **MEMBER** |
| **B** | ″ | `discord_buzz_digest.py:357` | **the scheduled board**, 7×/session | ⛔ **BACKGROUND** |

### ⛔ The consequence, and it is the whole of 3.4

**Neither `produce_chart` nor `_render_uncached` takes a class argument today.** Under `RenderGate`'s
signature — `acquire(..., cls=MEMBER)` — every one of those seven paths would default to **MEMBER**,
including all three background ones. ⭐ **A drop-in substitution at line 67 would therefore be inert
for the exact case C-09 exists to fix**, while every race in `c09_gate_races.py` stayed green,
because the races construct their contenders' classes directly and never go through these functions.

That is a rail gap I can name precisely: the races prove **the gate**, and nothing yet proves **the
call sites pass the right class**. The D-06 work is two threads of a parameter, and the rail that
must arrive with it is a source-level one — *for each of the seven rows above, assert the class that
site passes* — with a control proving it can see a wrong one.

### ⚠️ The warm cycle is the adversary, and it is ON by default

| fact | where |
|---|---|
| `DISCORD_CHART_HOTWARM_ENABLED` defaults to `"1"` | `api/main.py:806` |
| it waits **`MULTI_SLOT_WAIT_S = 25.0`** seconds for a slot | `discord_interactions.py:1334`, used at `:1630` |
| every render it makes already says `X-Render-Priority: background` | `warm_hot_charts:1563` wraps in `render_ids.background()`; the header is set at `discord_render/ids.py:80` |

⭐ **The priority it already declares is on the wrong side of the bottleneck** — the renderer honours
it; the web-side semaphore that decides who may *call* the renderer has never seen it. A warm render
happy to sit for 25 s is, against a plain semaphore, indistinguishable from a member.

### ⚠️ `/buzz` is the case that proves the class cannot be inferred from the call site

Site **B** serves a member command *and* a scheduler job **through one function**. So the class must
be **threaded from the caller** — `render_board_png(window, *, cls=...)` → `_render_uncached`. Any
attempt to infer it inside `buzz_image` (a thread name, a flag, "is a scheduler running") is the
second-authority defect this programme keeps re-committing. ⛔ And the failure is silent in the
dangerous direction: infer wrong and the scheduled board outranks a waiting member seven times a
session, which no test would notice.

### ⛔ What is NOT in this delta, deliberately

- **Preemption.** A background render already *holding* a slot is never taken off it. `chart-renderer`
  exposes no cancellation, so a "cancelled" render keeps running and still occupies a renderer slot
  while we stop waiting for its result — we would pay the cost and lose the result. The member's wait
  is bounded by **one** in-flight render instead of by the warm queue behind it, which was the part
  that actually hurt.
- **`BARS_WARM_SLOTS`** (`:96`) and **`_HEAL_SLOTS`** (`:1666`) are *different* semaphores bounding
  *different* resources (a cold bars fetch; how many heals may run). Neither is the renderer valve
  and neither is in scope. ⚠️ Naming them here because "there is a semaphore in this file" is how a
  future change lands on the wrong one.
