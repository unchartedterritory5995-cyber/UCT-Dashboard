## What changed (`api/live_massive_router.py`, `f7194160b`)

- `_flow_dates_all()` caches the all-source distinct-date scan for 60 s. In the pod that scan
  is 1,993–2,094 ms and used to run inside `_build_by_contract` on every single-ticker call —
  four times for a name widening through the ladder.
- `_contract_has_sweep_map` predicates on `+Symbol=?` so the planner takes the date index.
  SPY: 4,175 ms → 174 ms in the pod.

## The two timeouts are boot contention, not the change

flow-worker finished deploying at 07:07:20 CT. Its log shows the OPRA consumer prewarming
the ER cache for 3,615 symbols at 07:10:03 and the metadata cache for 2,744 symbols at
07:10:40; the in-pod probe at 07:11 read a one-minute load average of **97**. SPY and QQQ were
the first two requests into that pod. Re-probed at 07:11 they answered in 4.9 s and 2.6 s.
ACI's 10.8 s in the sweep was the same window; in the pod its all-history computation is
493 ms.

⚠️ **Member-facing consequence, recorded rather than fixed:** for the first ~3 minutes after a
flow-worker deploy, `/flow` can exceed the pre-V2 path's 30 s fetch budget and answer with the
"no answer in 30 s" sentence. Flow-worker deploys are rare (narrow watch list) and the same
window already costs members the OPRA tape gap, so the honest mitigation is the existing rule:
flow-worker pushes after hours only.
