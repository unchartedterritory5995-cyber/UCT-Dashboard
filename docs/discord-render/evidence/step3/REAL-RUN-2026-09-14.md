# Step 3 `--real` — 2026-09-14, after the close

**The first end-to-end S2 numbers this programme has ever had.** Everything before tonight ran
`--symbols stub` through a zero-cost handler: no symbol resolved, no bars fetched, no chart
rendered. Those were S1 numbers wearing an S2 label. These are not.

**Flag state:** the harness sets `DISCORD_RENDER_V2_ENABLED=1` **in its own process** and drives
`commands.handle` directly. Production `web` is unchanged and members are still on the pre-V2 path.
**Organic members exposed: 0.**

⚠️ **The wire hop is NOT in these numbers, deliberately.** `DELIVER_RATE_DEFAULT` is **1 write per
second**, and phase 3.1a alone queues ~600 jobs against a 180 s drain — delivering them would have
taken ten minutes and the result would have been a measurement of Discord's rate limiter wearing an
S2 label. That is the throttle *being* the measurement rather than part of it. The load phases
therefore run without `--deliver-channel` (the script says so on its own first line), and the wire
hop is measured separately at a rate the throttle can actually sustain.

**601 jobs over ten minutes, every one of them a real symbol, real bars and a real rendered chart.**

⛔ **The eight failures are ALL `queue_full`** — `{"queue_full": 8}`. Nothing timed out, nothing got
stuck, no breaker tripped, nothing exceeded 15 s. So the S5 miss is eight interactions that arrived
while the render queue was momentarily full, each answered immediately and honestly.

⚠️ **A queue filling at 1 arrival/second, when p95 is 1.7 s, needs a transient competitor.** The
warm cycle was observed all evening doing exactly that — `hot warm hit its 20s budget after 61.3s —
8 chart(s) deferred`, repeatedly. That is **C-09**, and it is the obvious suspect. ⛔ **It is a
suspect and not a finding**: this run did not instrument what else held a render slot at the moment
each of those eight arrived, so the link is unproven. Naming it as proven would be the third time
this programme mistook a plausible chain for a measured one.

## ⛔ THE HOP IS NAMED, AND IT IS NOT LATENCY

```
"failures_by_class": {"queue_full": 81}     # 3.1a
"failures_by_class": {"queue_full": 31}     # 3.1b
```

**Every single failure in both phases is `queue_full`.** Not a timeout, not a render error, not a
breaker trip — the bounded queue refusing excess work with an immediate, named refusal. That is the
design doing exactly what it was built to do at a load far beyond its capacity, and it is why S5
reads 35.7 %: those 81 interactions were *answered*, honestly and instantly, with "the queue is
full". The contract counts them as failures because the member did not get a chart.

⭐ **`acks_over_3s` is 0 in BOTH phases.** S1 — the SLO that says Discord must never time out an
interaction — held perfectly at 30 arrivals/second and through a 100-interaction burst. The ack
path is off the loop and it shows.

⭐ **So the honest sentence is: under saturation the system refuses cleanly and answers fast; the
jobs that get through take ~14–19 s end to end.** "S2 NOT MET" is true and is not the whole fact.

## ⚠️ AND THE LOAD IS NOT THE LOAD THAT WAS SPECIFIED — OI-37

The brief says **"30 concurrent → 100 burst → 1/s × 10 min"**. The harness's `--rate` is
**arrivals per second** (`--rate 30 --seconds 20` = ~600 interactions), and `step3_real.sh` passes
`--rate 30`. *30 concurrent* and *30 per second* are different tests: with ~15 s jobs, 30
concurrent is roughly **2 arrivals/second**, not 30.

⛔ So 3.1a measured a load about **fifteen times** the one that was asked for, against a guild of
1,558 members where nothing like either rate has ever been observed. The numbers are real; the
label "30 concurrent" on them is not. **3.1c (1/s × 10 min) is the realistic-rate phase and is the
number a flip decision should be read against.**

## ⚠️ THE CACHE DID NOT ENGAGE AT ALL — OI-38

```
"cache": {"hits": 0, "misses": 0, "hit_rate": null, "l2_promotions": 0}
```

Not "a poor hit rate" — **not even a miss**, in both phases. The cache path was never reached,
across ~76 real renders of only 20 distinct symbols, where a working cache should have been mostly
hits. The harness *does* call `enable_v2()`, so this is not the pre-V2 path, and `step3_real.sh`
exports `RENDER_CACHE_ENABLED=1`.

⛔ **No mechanism is asserted here.** What is established is the consequence: **Gap 1's 80 % hit
rate is a bench number and has NOT been reproduced under `--real`.** Until it is, the cache must not
be counted as contributing to any S2 figure, and the flip packet's cache row should say so.
