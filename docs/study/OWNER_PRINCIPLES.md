## A. The chart

1. **Always the member's own UCT chart**: the Charts-tab widget with their own settings, averages and volume, and our
   logo. Never a generic or rebuilt chart. *"use the UCT Charts widget function and our charts with the user's settings
   for each chart instead of this generic"* · *"not our complete and perfect UCT charts that each user has in their
   settings"* · *"idk why we are recreating charts instead of just importing our widget"*. No indicators blanked out
   (*"I dont like that we are asking questions about stuff with important indicators blank"*).
2. **Big, and it fits.** The chart is the hero, sized to the page, with 1 or 2 candles of space at the right edge
   (*"just like 1-2 candles space at max"*). Everything fits the screen; nothing important needs a scroll
   (*"use the space better of this page so i dont have to scroll a ton to click certain buttons"*).
3. **The chart teaches.** Levels, entry, stop, targets, labels and the action items are drawn on the chart, so the
   member sees the why, the what and the where by looking at it. *"the visual graphic of the chart when it does the
   tick replay can also list the levels and action items in the chart as well ... they can see it all by looking at
   the chart"* · *"Show the entry as a line and explain why and where and how simply"* · *"better graphics and on screen
   explanations of levels"*.
4. **Pointers that look designed.** A high-quality arrow and label placed in open space, never over candles, with a
   highlight that does not hide the candle's colour. *"interactive arrows or highlights of the candle"* · *"an arrow
   that is logically spaced to the left and pointing at the candle"* · *"ensure that it doesnt interfere with candles"*
   · *"I dont like it highlighted so i cant see the color"*.
5. **Replays are a lesson.** Tick by tick inside each day (30-minute or hourly), slower on the days that matter and
   quicker between them, starting near the action, running to the full culmination of the trade; no flashing. *"tick by
   tick so each day has multiple ticks"* · *"in between the main lesson days can be a bit faster"* · *"we should do to
   the full culmination or maximization of the trade"* · *"the replay starts way too far back"*.

## B. Teaching

6. **Never wrong.** *"We cannot have incorrect examples and explanations!"* Every example is checked against every setup
   before it plays; every number is read from bars.
7. **Examples a trader would want.** Liquid, sizable, known stocks in orderly spots (a flag, a double bottom, a wedge,
   the end of a downtrend); not news-driven, not a random big red day before; a failure only when it is labelled as a
   failed-setup lesson. Hundreds of examples, never the same three again. *"I want it in good spots on good stocks"* ·
   *"Location matters big time"* · *"We will need hundreds if not thousands of examples"*.
8. **Teach the trade, with numbers.** Entry, stop, sizing, trim, trail and the advance, on his rules. *"give some entry
   and sizing indications or entry tactics"* · *"I would rather have Entry, Stop, Trim, Trail, Advance"* · Kicker: entry
   *"over previous day high or on day 1 kicker ORB on the 30 minute"*, stop *"under the kicker candle low or entry with
   a 3.5% stop"*. Every other setup's entry, stop, trims (2R, 3R) and trail come from the app's own detectors
   (*"Those answers are out there. Either in the app, somewhere, or on the internet"*, 2026-11-08): `docs/study/TRADE_PLANS.md`.
9. **A mentor's voice, simply.** Short, direct, easy to read; the numbers a trader uses (*"% down from highs and %
   extended from MAs"*), not bar counts or jargon (*"dont need the word body"*), not tables of figures (*"The table of
   data and numbers I dont like really"*). *"simpler to read and more direct as a teacher and mentor"* · *"easier to read
   while getting a really good message across"*.
10. **Questions that ask, not lead,** with enough detail to know what to look at. *"that question is very leading"* ·
    *"make the question a bit more detailed"*.
11. **Our name.** Never "the firm" (*"quit calling us THE FIRM"*, said twice). UCT, or "we".
12. **Plain names, and every setup.** Call a mode what it is, and a game covers every setup we teach, not one: *"SPOT THE
    KICKER SHOULD be SPOT THE SETUP"* (2026-11-08; it was "Trap", then "Spot the Kicker"). Start a mode with a short
    tutorial (*"it should begin with a tutorial"*).

## C. Production

13. **A real production, not AI-coded.** Designer-level graphics, type and motion, as good as the best apps.
    *"I want the app to be a real production"* · *"feels very AI coded vs unique and skilled work"* · *"Study high quality
    apps"* · *"increase the quality of the production by 3X"* · *"have a graphic designer and web developer and visual
    expert and app builder and aesthetics professional take a look"* · *"I want a really cool production man!"*
14. **Fun, interactive, game-like**, with a real reward system. *"more interactive and fun like duolingo"* · *"improve
    the reward system and scoring of the app a lot more"*.
15. **The answer takes over the screen**, like a game. *"it should take over the entire screen as like a pop up
    uncovered answer like a normal app or game or test would have it"*.
16. **On brand, everywhere.** Black and gold, our type, our animated 3D logo (spinning, bouncing), never a cartoon
    character. *"more ON BRAND"* · *"change it to just a throbbing and bouncing version of our logo ... a 3D version that
    slowly spins"* · *"I want the logo ... to be moving in our animation work"*.
17. **Buttons worth pressing.** Start, Check and Next have been ranked Weak or Cut in every round; they must be a
    distinctive, premium control. *"the START BUTTON can be significantly better and cleaner"* · *"Check: horrible and
    low quality visual"*.
18. **Calm motion.** No bouncing text, no throbbing gold round the chart, no flashing; smooth, purposeful moves. Pick
    one presentation; no awkward switch between two (*"pick one or the other"*).
21. **One design, every mode** (added 2026-11-08). The lesson player, Blitz, Boss, Spot the Setup and Hard mode wear the
    same design, the one built with him in Hard mode: the start arena, the status line, the chart as the hero with the
    designed pointer, the question header, the answer tiles, the verdict line and stamp, the lesson card, the one Check
    and Next bar, and the coin end screen. A new mode starts from these pieces. *"This is a different design than the ones
    we have worked on together!!!"* · *"Need all the design templates and graphics to be uniform throughout each
    section!"*
