# Study training corpus — what in the education module trains the brain

Owner, 2026-10-27: "I want to make sure all of this feedback and information is stored because I'm going to use it
to continue to train our brain as well as build our teaching." Then, the same day: "Everything though, all the
entirety of the teaching and education module here can be used to train the brain and full system of analysis and
recognition etc."

**Standing rule.** The whole Study module is training material for the firm's own analysis-and-recognition system
(the "brain"), not only the chart rulings. Every file below is kept in git, in plain text, with the owner's words
verbatim and the chart facts beside them, so a later training pass can trace any rule to the sentence and the chart
it came from. Nothing in this corpus is written by a model as a market fact; the owner rules, the code records.

## 4. Member behaviour (lower weight, aggregate only)

Pod tables `study_attempts`, `study_item_state`, `study_skill`, `study_profile`, `study_sessions`: what members answer,
where they stumble, which items are too easy or too hard. Use in aggregate (item-level difficulty, confusion pairs),
never as a source of market rules, and never exported with user ids.

## Not part of the corpus

- `docs/notebook/user-study/` is a sample member notebook used to build the notebook feature, not the owner's teaching.
- Anything a model wrote as a market fact. Draft items (`review_status = draft`) are candidates, not teaching, until ruled.

## How to assemble a training pass

1. Export the pod tables in §1 and §2 (`study_feedback`, `study_corrections`, approved `study_items`, their `study_bars`)
   to `docs/study/audits/<date>/` with the scratchpad `run_pod_py.ps1` (read-only).
2. Rebuild `review_images.json` so every ruled chart has its image (`build_review_index.py`).
3. Join: ruling (§1) -> item -> bars -> finder facts (§3) -> lesson text (§2). The owner's words are the label; the facts
   and bars are the features; the lesson text is the explanation.
4. Keep the `rule` lines and `source_ruling` lines as the definition set; everything else is examples of them.
