# Voice and likeness consent (template)

**Status:** Phase 0 template. No voice sample is used, uploaded or cloned until the owner has
written the consent below in his own words (a short message in chat or this file signed and
committed is enough).

## What is being asked

1. **Voice clone.** A synthetic voice built from recordings of the owner (Desk sessions on
   YouTube and/or a clean sample recorded for this purpose), used only to narrate Study intro
   videos (about 100-120 clips of 30-60 seconds).
2. **Stylized animated likeness.** An animated host based on the owner's appearance, used in the
   same intro videos where it adds something.

## Terms the owner is agreeing to (edit freely)

- The clone and the likeness are used **only for UCT Intelligence's Study intro videos** and for
  nothing else. No member-facing use outside those videos without a new consent.
- **Every script is read and approved by the owner before it is rendered.** Nothing is generated
  from text the owner has not seen.
- The voice model and any source sample are stored only in the toolchain account the owner
  controls, and can be **deleted on request** at any time.
- The owner can **revoke** this consent at any time; existing videos are then re-narrated or
  removed.
- No third party is given the voice model.
