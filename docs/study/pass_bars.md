# Pass bars — proposal for the owner

**Status:** Phase 0 proposal (2026-10-17). The owner asked to review every number. Nothing is
locked by these bars (owner decision: no locks); they decide badges, "passed" marks and Gold.

| Thing | Proposed bar | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A **Learn** rung (1-3) | 70% (7 of 10) | Rung 1 is walk-throughs (unscored); 2-3 are first scored reps. Low bar so nobody stalls at the start. |
| A **Practise** rung (4-6) | 80% | The dossier's standard checkpoint bar. |
| A **Prove** rung (7-9) | 90% | Application on real charts or real numbers; the band that earns Gold. |
| **Test out** of a unit | 90% on one 10-item set at Prove difficulty, one attempt per unit per day | One shot a day so it cannot be brute-forced. |
| **Module checkpoint** (badge) | 80% on a 20-item set, 30% of which is recycled from earlier modules | The dossier's 80% + its recycling rule. |
| **Size module checkpoint** | **10 of 10**, no partial credit | The dossier's deliberate exception; the toolkit card says the same. |
| **Mastered** band on a skill | ≥ 80 on the recency-weighted score, minimum 8 attempts | Spec §4.4. |
| **Gold** on a skill | ≥ 95 with ≥ 40 reps | The "40 logged reps" rule; rare by design. |
| **Fresh** | last attempt on the skill within 30 days | Spec §4.4. |
| Streak day | Today's Study finished (10 of 10 answered, any score) | Showing up is what the streak measures. |
| Placement test | 15 items; a track is marked "tested out to rung 6" at ≥ 80% on its items | Optional; only moves the recommended node. |

**Open for the owner:** raise or lower any row; whether Prove should be 90 or 85; whether a
checkpoint can be retaken the same day (proposed: next day).
