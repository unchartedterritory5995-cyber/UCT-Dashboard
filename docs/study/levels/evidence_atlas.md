# Levels evidence: candidate rules replayed on the deep scan (every clean, institutional instance since 1995, worked or failed)

Generated by `docs/study/levels/measure_rules.py` (read-only). The instances are every one the owner's calibrated finders accepted in the top 1,000 stocks by dollar volume that day since 1995, with no other setup firing on the same bar, sampled evenly up to 800 per setup, and NOT filtered for having worked: this is the closest thing to the setup's real edge the data allows (still only stocks listed today: delisted names are missing). Daily bars cannot order the moves inside a day, so the stop is not checked on the fill day, and the stop day's high is not counted.

Columns: **n** examples the rule applied to · **risk** median stop distance (% of entry) · **stop hit** the initial stop was hit at some point in 60 sessions, even after a gain (nothing trails yet; the sell rules are his to make) · **1R / 2R / 3R** reached that multiple of the risk before any stop · **best R** median best move in R before the stop · **+20 sessions** median return 20 sessions after the fill, trades not stopped.
