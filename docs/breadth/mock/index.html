Desktop, OLED, 2 years

  • One control row; menus open over the page, so nothing pushes the plot down (A-17, A-18).
  • The reading line states what, when and how fresh in a sentence (A-15).
  • Two panels, each on its own real scale; no second y-axis (A-05). Legend rows sit on their panel; values are tabular.
  • S&P 500 is recorded from Jan 2, 2026 — the unrecorded span is shaded and named, not blank (A-10).
  • The record strip under the plot shows reconstructed vs collected sessions (A-10) — the tab's one bold element.
  • Year-aware ticks on the shared axis (A-06); range pills including 2Y (A-20).
  • Chrome colours derived from tokens: axis text ≥ 4.5:1 (A-14).
