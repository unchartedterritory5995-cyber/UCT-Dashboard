## ⚰️ What it was — and it was NOT the product

An earlier draft of this record (committed in `68872b3e0`/`73a4286d0`) called this "a
regression against the hub's own stated design" and pointed at `HubContext.jsx:185-189`.
**That was wrong, and it was wrong in the way this repo keeps paying for: an instrument
reported a property of itself.** Three measurements settled it:

1. **Local build, same phone-class context, same routes:** hub present and showing on all
   seven routes probed (`/dashboard`, `/model-book`, `/charts`, `/desk`, `/journal`,
   `/community`, `/screener`), box 84×84, zero page/console errors.
2. **Production, warm pod, same synthetic account, same route order, 6 s settle per
   route:** hub present and showing on every one of the six, plus `/support` and a second
   `/model-book`; `/api/auth/me` 200 throughout; landed URLs all as requested.
3. **The same tool, re-run on production at 01:08Z (warm pod):** `TOUCH PASS OK — 19
   routes … the hub mounted on every route the registry says it should`, console errors 0 on
   18 routes (3 pre-existing 404s on `/desk`).

**Mechanism.** The touch probe did `page.goto(route, wait_until="domcontentloaded")` and then
a FIXED `wait_for_timeout(2500)` before reading the DOM. Sixty seconds after a deploy the pod
is cold and every hashed chunk is an edge-cache miss; the six routes that read "NO HUB" are
the six heaviest lazy pages, still fetching their chunk at 2.5 s. While a route's chunk is
pending, the app's route-level `<Suspense>` replaces the WHOLE `<Routes>` tree — Layout and
`<HubRoot/>` included — with its fallback, so `[data-testid="hub-root"]` is genuinely absent
at that instant. The tool's sentence "Eligibility is viewport+pointer, not route-shaped, so
this is a real absence" was true about eligibility and false about what it had measured.
It is the "sampling where a waiter was available" class (CLAUDE.md), and the same
cold-boot timing produced the `/options-flow` page-load timeout in the desktop smoke that
night (reproduced on two different builds at +30 s, gone at +5 min).

**Tool fixed in the same push:** the touch probe now WAITS for the hub root, bounded by
`HUB_MOUNT_BOUND_MS` (20 s), before it may call an absence real, and its failure text says to
re-run on a warm pod if the pod is under a minute old. `--self-check` still passes (the
present-is-not-showing control is untouched).

## The one finding from this run that WAS real

`GET /api/barspack/manifest` answered 401 on every route (the "console errors: 2" on the good
routes). The barspack routes had been gated by `require_bars_access` since `2d121371f`
(2026-09-13) while `app/src/lib/barsPackClient.js` still fetched with `credentials: 'omit'`,
so the Universe Bars Pack had been silently dead for every member for eleven days. Fixed as
`73a4286d0` (via `68872b3e0`, an H15 rollback `5fd248c40`, and a re-land); the warm-pod
re-run above shows 0 barspack lines in the console.
